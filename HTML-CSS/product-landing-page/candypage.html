Our History

The Garage Band Beginning (1990)

The story of Higgins & Sons doesn’t begin in a quaint, old-world kitchen, but in a damp garage in the Pacific Northwest in the fall of 1990. Arthur Higgins was a broke bass player for a struggling grunge band. While trying to brew a cheap batch of root beer for a backyard concert using an old copper home-brewing kit, Arthur accidentally left the burner on high while his band played a chaotic, two-hour set.

By the time they finished, the liquid had boiled down into a thick, crystalline sheet of rich, sassafras-infused hard candy. Desperate to recoup their grocery money, the band broke the sheet into shards with a drumstick and sold them to the concert-goers as "Grunge Glass." It was an instant hit, and Arthur quickly realized he was a much better candy maker than he was a bass player.
